hero.webp
Original cinematic panoramic hero artwork for the independent literary horror bookstore Midnight Books. Wide 3:1 composition, charcoal black sky at night, a huge dusty crimson-vermilion moon at x=75% behind long black spires and crooked gables of a Victorian manor, tiny lit upper windows, gnarled woods on hills, receding layers of low mist and etched silhouettes. The left 45 percent is nearly solid very dark negative space for HTML title and button; scene and moon stay right and merge gently with black left. A graphic and extraordinarily atmospheric original late 19th-century horror frontispiece printed in two inks on subtly toothy paper, fine engraved hatch marks. Strong near-black/dark muted red, expressive but restrained; no typography, text, signature, margins or border.
